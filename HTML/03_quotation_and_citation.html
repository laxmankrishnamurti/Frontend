blockquote : element defines a section that is quoted from another source.

From Wikipedia about Climate Change :
Present-day climate change includes both global warming—the ongoing increase in global average temperature—and its wider effects on Earth's climate system. In a broader sense, climate change also includes previous long-term changes to Earth's climate.

q : defines a short quotation. Browser normally insert quation marks around the quotation. It dosen't have any cite attribute.

Example:

My Observation : Life is not about choices it's all about the chooser.

abbr : defines an abbreviation or an acronym. Marking abbreviations can give useful information to browsers, translation systems and search-engine.

Example :

The WHO was founded in 1948.

address : defines the contact information for the author/owner of a document or an article.
